Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: Catakig X does not collect, store, or share any information about you. Everything the app works with stays on your Mac.

No data collection

  • Catakig X collects no personal data, usage data, diagnostics, or identifiers.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not connect to the internet or any network service. It has no network access at all.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, or in-app purchases.

What stays on your Mac

Catakig X is a sandboxed Mac App Store app. The files it keeps for itself live only in its own sandbox folder on your Mac (~/Library/Containers/com.rodneythacker.CatakigX):

  • ROM files you import with Import ROMs…
  • Your preferences
  • The automatically saved state of your last Apple II session
  • Recovered copies of disks that couldn't be saved back in their original format
  • A log of the error messages Catakig X has shown, and any crash or hang reports macOS gives the app, kept for bug reports

Disk images, saved machine states, printouts and screen images are read from and saved to the places you choose, and are only opened when you choose them. Catakig X remembers where your disks were so it can reopen them, using standard macOS security-scoped bookmarks that also stay on your Mac. None of this is ever sent anywhere. If you choose Save Bug Report… in the Help menu, Catakig X saves a file holding the Apple II's state and screen, the error log, and your Mac's model and macOS version (and, if you leave that option checked, the disks in the drives) to a place you choose; it is never sent automatically, and goes to the developer only if you send it yourself. To remove it all, quit Catakig X and delete the folder above.

Devices and permissions

Catakig X uses Bluetooth and USB only so you can play with a game controller connected to your Mac, and printing only when you choose to print. The included Quick Look extension shows previews of disk images on your Mac and does not send them anywhere.

Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports and usage statistics for Catakig X to the developer. That sharing is controlled by you and Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy, and Catakig X itself plays no part in it. Purchases and downloads are handled by the Mac App Store.

Children

Catakig X doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
